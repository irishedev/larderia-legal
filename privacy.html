Overview

Cairn Studio (“we”, “us”) operates the Larderia mobile app. Larderia helps households track food inventory. Current versions store all inventory on your device. There is no cloud account or multi-device sync.

Contact: support@cairn-studio.com

Information we collect

  • Inventory data — items, quantities, storage locations, consumption and waste logs, categories, allergens, and nutrition preferences you enter. This stays on your device unless you export a backup file.
  • Household data — household name, address (if you provide it), roles, and managed dependent profiles on this device.
  • Camera (optional) — used only for barcode scanning and on-device text recognition (nutrition labels, receipts). Images are processed on your device.
  • Product images — when you scan a barcode, we may display a product thumbnail URL from Open Food Facts; we do not store that image on our servers.
  • Notifications — local reminders on your device. We do not collect push tokens for a cloud messaging service.
  • Health data (optional) — if you opt in, nutrition information from logged consumption may be written to Apple Health or Health Connect on your device.
  • Location (optional) — used only to help set your household address when you choose to use that feature.

How we use information

We use this data to provide inventory tracking, reports, local reminders, and optional health export. We do not sell your personal information.

Where data is stored

  • On this device — inventory and settings are stored in the app on your phone. Your operating system may include app data in device backups (for example iCloud Backup or Android Auto Backup).
  • Backups you export — if you export a backup from Settings, that file is yours to store or share. We do not receive a copy unless you email it to support.

Crash, diagnostics, performance, and analytics

Current store builds do not send crash reports, performance traces, or analytics to Google Firebase or another diagnostics vendor. If the app crashes, your device vendor may collect a crash log according to your OS settings.

Third-party services

  • Open Food Facts — barcode product lookups. We send barcode numbers to look up product information; we do not send your inventory lists.

Data retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you uninstall the app, clear app storage, or restore over it. You may export a backup from Settings.

See our data deletion page or email support@cairn-studio.com.

Children’s privacy

Larderia is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do.

Contact
