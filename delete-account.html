On your device

Larderia does not create a cloud account. Inventory lives on this phone. To remove it:

  1. Optionally export a backup from Settings
  2. Uninstall the app, or clear the app’s storage in system settings

Email

If you need help deleting data, email us. We do not hold a copy of your inventory unless you sent a backup file.

Email support@cairn-studio.com

What is deleted

  • On-device inventory, household, and settings when you uninstall or clear storage
  • Any backup file you stored elsewhere, if you delete that file yourself
